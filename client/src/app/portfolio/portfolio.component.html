Portfolio Diversification Guide & Methodology
Diversification Classification Levels
1: Concentrated High Risk
Number of Sectors: 1-3 Sectors
High risk portfolio with limited sector exposure. Vulnerable to sector-specific downturns.
Recommendation: Add positions in different sectors to reduce risk.
2: Moderately Diversified Moderate Risk
Number of Sectors: 4-5 Sectors
Reasonable diversification with moderate risk reduction. Still susceptible to sector concentration risk.
Recommendation: Consider adding 1-2 more sectors for better risk distribution.
3: Well Diversified Low Risk
Number of Sectors: 6+ Sectors
Optimal diversification with exposure across multiple sectors. Reduced portfolio volatility and sector-specific risk.
Recommendation: Maintain current diversification while monitoring sector weightings.
Key Portfolio Metrics
Sector Count: Number of different sectors represented in your portfolio
Sector Allocation: Percentage of total portfolio value in each sector
Concentration Risk: Risk from over-weighting in specific sectors
Academic Foundation

Our diversification methodology is based on Modern Portfolio Theory and sector diversification principles established in academic literature.

Primary Reference:
Markowitz, H. (1952). "Portfolio Selection." The Journal of Finance, 7(1), 77-91.
Sector Diversification:

Risk Reduction:
Evans, J. L., & Archer, S. H. (1968). "Diversification and the reduction of dispersion: an empirical analysis." The Journal of Finance, 23(5), 761-767.
